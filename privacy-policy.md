# Privacy Policy for Wacky Garpo

**Last updated: September 28, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Wacky Garpo ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Wacky Garpo mobile game.

Wacky Garpo is developed and published by Nguyen Huy Duc.

## 1. Information We Collect

Wacky Garpo does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to directly provide personal information such as your name, phone number, or postal address.

However, the Game uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain information about your device and your use of the Game.

This information may include:

- Device and app identifiers
- Device type, operating system, and app version
- General or approximate location derived from information such as IP address
- Game usage and interaction data, such as game sessions, scores, retries, gameplay events, and engagement
- Advertising data and interactions with advertisements
- Crash logs, diagnostic information, performance data, and relevant application/device state
- Other technical information necessary to provide, secure, analyze, and improve the Game and advertising services

## 2. How We Use Information

Information collected through the Game may be used to:

- Operate and maintain Wacky Garpo
- Understand how players use the Game
- Analyze gameplay, engagement, and game performance
- Diagnose crashes, errors, and technical problems
- Improve gameplay, stability, and user experience
- Display, measure, and manage advertising
- Prevent fraud, abuse, and security issues
- Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

## 3. Third-Party Services

Wacky Garpo uses services provided by Google, including:

### Google Firebase Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how players interact with the Game. This may include analytics information and gameplay events such as game starts, game overs, retries, scores, and score milestones.

### Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to identify and diagnose crashes and technical problems. Crashlytics may process crash logs, diagnostic information, device metadata, application state, and identifiers used for crash reporting.

### Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, advertising data, ad interactions, diagnostic information, and other information necessary to provide and measure advertising.

Depending on your location, applicable law, device settings, and your consent choices, advertisements may be personalized, non-personalized, or limited.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. Consent and Advertising Choices

Where required by applicable law, Wacky Garpo may ask for your consent before certain data is used for advertising, analytics, or related purposes.

In regions where consent requirements apply, you may be provided with options to consent, decline consent, or manage your privacy choices.

You may also be able to change or withdraw certain consent choices through privacy options made available in the Game, where required.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information directly to third parties.

Information may be processed or shared with service providers, including Google and advertising technology providers, as necessary to provide analytics, crash reporting, advertising, fraud prevention, security, and related services.

These third-party providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies and applicable law.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to the retention policies and settings of those service providers.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the Game. We also rely on established third-party service providers to process certain information.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Wacky Garpo is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If we learn that personal information from a child has been collected in a manner that does not comply with applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to address the issue.

Advertising and data practices may also be adjusted where required by applicable laws or platform policies concerning children.

## 9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent.

Some information used by Wacky Garpo is processed through third-party services and may not directly identify you to us.

You may contact us regarding a privacy request using the contact information below.

## 10. International Data Processing

Third-party service providers used by Wacky Garpo may process information on servers located in countries other than the country where you live. Such processing is subject to applicable data protection requirements and the safeguards provided by those service providers.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add or change features, services, advertising practices, or legal requirements.

Any changes will be posted on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised accordingly.
